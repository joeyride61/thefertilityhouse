The Fertility House does not guarantee the acceptance or the denial of any surrogate on behalf of a designated participating fertility center. The Fertility House is providing a professional review based on educational experience and professional training; it is not intended to be medical advice or an opinion. The Fertility House is not responsible for the failure of any surrogate/gestational carrier candidate to be chosen by an intended parent or fertility center after medical records have been reviewed and a formal summary statement has been provided in the form of the Medical Record Review and Summary Statement document. Candidates should continue to be presented to other inquiring intended parents and fertility centers.

The terms surrogate and gestational carrier by definition have similarities but are different; for all intended purposes and for the purposes of screening legal medical documents, a surrogate and a gestational carrier are considered the same.

Upon a request to review medical records, all records for each pregnancy (including but not limited to all applicable prenatal, intranatal, and postnatal records) must be obtained and presented for review. Additional documents may be requested at any time during the review process. The surrogate/gestational carrier will be required to have a one-time phone consult with The Fertility House; the intent of the phone consult is for clarification purposes only.

Once all medical records have been sent to The Fertility House, payment must be collected prior to release of any final documentation including, but not limited to, the release of the Medical Record Review and Summary Statement.

Once Fees are collected, The Fertility House will work diligently to provide the Medical Record Review and Summary Statement in a time-sensitive manner of one to two weeks from the date stamped by original payment. A rush review can be requested for an additional fee.

If paper charts are obtained, they are the property of the surrogate/gestational carrier and will be returned at a later date or shredded in an acceptable manner. If records are to be returned, it will be at the cost of the agency or the original owner. All medical records will be kept confidential and released only with signed permission.

The Fertility House summary statement entitled Medical Record Review and Summary Statement expires after one year from its originating date or in the event of medical changes incurred by the surrogate/gestational carrier. In this case, an addendum can be provided for additional fees. The originating date to determine expiration can be found on the signature line of the original report.

All information about an inquiring intended parent, surrogacy agency, or surrogate/gestational carrier is considered confidential. Said surrogate/gestational carrier demographic and surrogacy agencies and their representatives may be referenced for statistics only to enable the growth of The Fertility House. Names will not be used.
