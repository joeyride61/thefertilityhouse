Why The Fertility House exists

Infertility is confusing, frustrating and unfair. Injections, consultations, glimmers of hope and heartbreaking disappointments. I know. I've been through all of it.

Before I was a provider, I was the patient.

Years of infertility made Jamie the nurse practitioner she is: someone who reads every chart like it's her own story.

Two houses, one journey

Before, during and after.

A family doesn't start at the transfer or end at the birth. The Fertility House handles the medical review. The Wellness House gets bodies ready for it, and helps them recover after.

Before

Get ready

Weight, blood sugar and hormones shape whether a body is ready to carry. Many agencies and clinics set a BMI limit for carriers, and women with PCOS often need regular cycles first.

The Wellness House
During

Get approved
