About Me

Who am I?

My name is Jamie Hainlen, MSN, FNP-C. I am a Board certified Family Nurse Practitioner. I hold a Bachelor's Degree in Business from Northern Arizona University and a Master’s Degree in Nursing from California Baptist University. I have over 9 years of experience and training in Reproductive Endocrinology and have trained with some of the very best top physicians in the OC area.

I have counseled with doctors, lawyers, and psychiatrists while overseeing hundreds of third-party cases (including both surrogates/gestational carriers and egg donors).

The service for the agency

Through years of education and experience, I developed an effective system for screening the medical records of surrogates and gestational carriers. After carefully reviewing your gestational carrier’s records, I will provide you with a Medical Record Review and Summary Statement you can share with the fertility clinic of your choice. You can also use this Summary Statement as an adjunct to the candidate's file for future intended parents. This will aid in a more thorough vetting of candidates with the idea that you will have a faster approval of your candidate with the fertility clinic; likewise it will offer you peace of mind that the candidate you are presenting is an ideal candidate for surrogacy.

The service for the gestational carrier

Are you a surrogate and looking for a family to work with? Well, you have come to the right place! For free, I will personally review your medical records and create a Medical Record Review and Summary Statement. I will represent you by connecting you to a top agency. I will also coach you along the way and provide all the support you need through your surrogacy journey.

Why do this?

Infertility is confusing, frustrating, and unfair. Injections, consultations, glimmers of hope, heartbreaking disappointments … I know. I’ve been through it all.

My husband and I struggled through years of infertility, unsuccessful egg donation retrieval, four rounds of IUI, two rounds of IVF, two failed embryo transfers, and two miscarriages. Finally, our little rainbow baby success story made us the parents we had always dreamt about becoming. I have experienced all of the pain and all of the joy from both personal and professional viewpoints.

My journey led to my passion to use what I’ve learned … to help families who want to have a baby.

Whether you are an agency needing medical records reviewed or an individual prospective gestational carrier, I’m so happy you are here and have found me! I’d love to connect and help you in this journey — one embryo one baby at a time, creating families and making dreams come true!
