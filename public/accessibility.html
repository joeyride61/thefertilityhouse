Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use The Fertility House and The Wellness House, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, magnification or other assistive technology.

These sites are built and tested to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. That includes readable text sizes and color contrast, labeled forms, full keyboard use with a visible focus outline, text descriptions for images, a skip link on every page, and a way to pause anything that moves. If your device is set to reduce motion, our scrolling animations turn off.

We test with automated tools and by hand, but no site is perfect. If something is hard to use, or you'd like information in another format, please tell us and we'll help.
