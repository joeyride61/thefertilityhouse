Doseday

Every dose, every refill, handled.

Doseday is the free tracker that comes with every Wellness House order. This demo shows it through the eyes of Will Hainlen, a member on tirzepatide who also takes BPC-157 he bought elsewhere.

  1. His orders link automatically. Will's Wellness House tirzepatide shows up in his stack with dose, units to draw and supply.
  2. He adds anything else. BPC-157 was added by hand, on the exact days he picked: Monday, Wednesday and Friday.
  3. Doseday sees the run-out coming. It knows he has one dose of tirzepatide left and tells him to reorder now, before there's a gap.
  4. One tap reorders the next batch. The order lands in Jamie's CRM for review. When it's delivered, Will adds it to his stack and the countdown resets.
