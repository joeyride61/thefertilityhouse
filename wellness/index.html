i. Why wellness lives at the Fertility House

Before I was a provider, I was the patient.

My husband and I spent years in fertility treatment: an unsuccessful egg donation retrieval, four IUIs, two rounds of IVF, two failed transfers and two miscarriages before our rainbow baby arrived.

Somewhere in those years I learned that the numbers on a lab report are never just numbers. Blood sugar, insulin, hormones, sleep and stress all decide what your body is ready for.

“Fertility taught me to read a body as a whole. Wellness is where I get to use that for everyone.”

ii. The connection

Metabolic health is fertility health.

Weight, insulin resistance and inflammation shape cycles, egg quality and pregnancy outcomes. The same tools that help with weight loss also help women prepare their bodies, on purpose and on a timeline.

Mixing guide

How to reconstitute a vial

Most of our vials arrive as a freeze-dried powder for stability. Mixing takes two minutes. Jamie walks every new patient through it on video before the first dose.

  1. Gather suppliesYour vial, bacteriostatic water, a 3 mL mixing syringe, U-100 insulin syringes and alcohol pads. All included in your kit.
  2. Clean both topsWash your hands. Flip off the caps and wipe both rubber stoppers with alcohol. Let them dry.
  3. Draw the waterPull the amount of bacteriostatic water on your prescription label into the mixing syringe.
  4. Add it slowly, down the sideAngle the needle so water runs down the glass, not onto the powder. Go slowly.
  5. Swirl, never shakeGently roll the vial between your palms until clear. Peptides are fragile; shaking can damage them.
  6. Label, refrigerate, use within 28 daysWrite the mix date on the label. Store at 2–8°C away from light.

Questions

Good to know

Something else on your mind?

Do I need a prescription?

Yes. Every program starts with an intake and provider review. If a medication isn't a safe fit, you aren't charged for it.

Why cash pay and no insurance?

Insurance rarely covers these programs and adds weeks of paperwork. One transparent monthly price covers medication, supplies and check-ins. HSA and FSA cards work.

I'm trying to conceive. Can I use a GLP-1?

GLP-1s are used to prepare before conception, not during. Semaglutide's label says to stop at least 2 months before a planned pregnancy. Jamie builds that timeline into your plan.

Who should not take a GLP-1?

Anyone pregnant or breastfeeding, or with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid cancer or MEN 2. A history of pancreatitis needs a careful conversation first.

Can I pause or cancel?

Anytime before your next refill ships. No contracts.
